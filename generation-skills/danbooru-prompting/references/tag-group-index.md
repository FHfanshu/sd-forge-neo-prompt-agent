# Tag group navigation index

Use this index to decide where a disputed term belongs, then consult the
live tag-group wiki (via `inspect_danbooru_wikis` on the `tag_group:*` title,
or danbooru.donmai.us/wiki_pages/tag_groups) for exact definitions, aliases,
implications, and exceptions. A term may belong to more than one group.

## Composition and style

Artistic license, image composition, backgrounds, censorship, character
count, colors, fine art parody, focus tags, lighting, prints, visual
aesthetic, patterns, symbols, text, Japanese dialects, year tags.
→ `tag_group:image_composition`

## Body and appearance

Body parts, ass, breasts tags, face tags, ears tags, eyes tags, hair, hair
color, hair styles, hands, gestures, feet, neck and neckwear, posture, pussy,
shoulders, skin color, wings, injury.
→ `tag_group:body_parts`

## Clothing and accessories

Accessories, attire, handwear, headwear, legwear, neck and neckwear, sexual
attire, sleeves, embellishment, eyewear, fashion style, makeup, covering,
nudity, mask, swimsuit.
→ `tag_group:attire`

## Actions, sex, and relationships

Sex acts, simulated sex acts, sexual positions, BDSM and torture, dances,
family relationships, groups, phrases, verbs and gerunds, transgender,
gender nonconformity.
→ `tag_group:sex_acts`

## Objects and environments

Audio tags, holding tags, cards, doors and gates, piercings, sex objects,
weapons, vehicles, ships, aircraft, armor, technology, fire, water,
locations, real-world locations.
→ `tag_group:holding_tags`

## Creatures, plants, food, and activities

Animals, birds, cats, dogs, legendary creatures, flowers, food tags, board
games, sports, video games, game activities.
→ `list_of_animals`

## Themes and real world

Companies and brand names, holidays and celebrations, jobs, people, history,
theme, subjective, technology.
→ `tag_group:theme`

Note: `subjective` is a navigation group, not permission to apply
opinion-based tags in ordinary image tagging.

## Characters, media, and meta

Character lists are organized by copyright, including common series such as
Arknights, Fate, Genshin-related media, Pokemon, Touhou, Umamusume, Vocaloid,
and many others. Meta groups include metatags and drawing software.
→ `tag_groups` (live index)

## Reminders

- A `tag_group:*` page title is not itself a generation tag.
- Search results and uninspected references are not evidence — inspect the
  page before citing it.
- Stop expanding references when the evidence answers the task.
